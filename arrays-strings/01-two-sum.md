## Problem: Two Sum

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a brute-force approach with two loops. For each element, I checked the elements after it to find a pair whose sum equals the target.

### Complexity

- **Time:** O(n²)
- **Space:** O(1) excluding the returned result

### Notes

I tested a typical case and an edge case locally before submitting the solution to LeetCode. The solution was Accepted.
