## Problem: Reverse a String

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach

I used two pointers, one at the beginning and one at the end of the string. I swapped the characters and moved the pointers toward the center.

### Complexity

- **Time:** O(n)
- **Space:** O(1)

### Notes

I tested the string "hello", which becomes "olleh", and the single-character string "a". The solution was Accepted.
